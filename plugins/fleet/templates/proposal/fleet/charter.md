# Charter

Drafted by the chief from the kickstart interview, and applied by you (`/fleet <n> apply`). Every instance reads it at
its start; only an applied change rewrites it.

## Goal

What the proposal must show, and to whom.

## Not in scope

## How we know it worked

Each measure with its target and how it's measured.

## Constraints

Budget, deadline, scarce resources, what must never leave this machine.

## Principals

Who besides the person may direct the work, and in what scope.

## Questions for the person

What running something (a test, a check) or reading the world (the shared files, the code, the findings, verify.md) can
settle is settled first, by whoever would ask; only decisions of taste, product and priority reach you.

## Outbound

Who the fleet may send anything to (by default, nobody but the person).

## Cadence

How often each kind of owner lands something; quiet hours; time zone.
