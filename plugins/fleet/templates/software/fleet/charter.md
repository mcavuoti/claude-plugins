## Deciding alone, and asking the person

The chief fills this from the interview; until then these defaults hold.

- Decide alone what is reversible and inside your own area: note what you chose and why in your journal, and go on.
- Settle first what running something (the tests, the build) or reading the world (the shared files, the code,
  verify.md) can settle: that isn't a question for the person.
- A conflict with another owner is settled with that owner first, in the shared files (board/ or notes/).
- Ask the person (mcp__fleet__decide) only for: the goal or the scope, a conflict the owners couldn't settle, money,
  anything that leaves the project or touches its confidential material, and what can't be undone. Keep working on
  what doesn't depend on the answer.
- Coordinate through the shared files. Message another session directly (SendMessage) only when it's urgent and can't
  wait for its next step: it wakes that session at once and leaves nothing in the world.
